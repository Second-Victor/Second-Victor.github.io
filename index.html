Downward is out now!

22nd October 2026

A little late posting this here, but Downward launched on the App Store back on the 15th of August.

It's a minimal Markdown editor for iPhone and iPad. Open files stored on your device or in iCloud, write in a clean and focused editor, and hide the Markdown syntax when you just want to read.

Downward is free. If you'd like to support it, the one-time supporter upgrade unlocks extra themes, importing and exporting custom themes, and importing your own fonts.

It has had a few small updates since launch and is now on version 1.0.3.

Download Downward on the App Store.
