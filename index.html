Downward the minimal markdown editor is near!

8th May 2026

Just about to launch my newest app Downward with these features:

  • Minimal Liquid Glass UI.
  • Browse local files on your device.
  • Open and edit files stored in iCloud.
  • Minimal Markdown editing for iPhone and iPad.
  • Tap to toggle tasks.
  • Long press to quickly copy text from code blocks.
  • Option to hide Markdown syntax for a cleaner look.
  • Simple interface designed around focus and readability.
  • Great for notes, drafts, documentation, journals and plain text workflows.

This is a free app but I have a supporters pack in-app purchase which unlocks themes and the importing of fonts.
