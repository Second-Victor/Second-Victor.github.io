When..

When.. records exactly when and where the moments in your life happen, from feeding the goldfish to the details of a car accident. Add notes, log several times and places against one event, and keep it all in sync with iCloud.

Downward

Downward is a calm, minimal Markdown editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Open files on your device or in iCloud Drive, write with the syntax shown or hidden, and make the editor your own with themes and fonts.

Done..

Done.. is a task list for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built on a todo.txt file you own. No account, no data collected.

Dinkus

Dinkus is a plain text editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac that colours your Markdown as you write. Coming soon to the App Store..
