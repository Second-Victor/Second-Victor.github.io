Downward

A clean, minimal Markdown editor for iPhone and iPad.

Browse local on-device or iCloud files, customise your writing space, and hide syntax for a calmer look.

Downward is a minimal Markdown editor for iPhone and iPad, built for focused writing, simple file browsing and a cleaner way to work with Markdown.

Browse and edit Markdown files stored locally on your device or in iCloud, without locking your writing into a complicated workspace. Downward keeps your files accessible while giving you a calm, customisable editor for notes, drafts, documentation, journals, writing projects and everyday text editing.

The editor is designed to feel simple and quiet. You can work with Markdown syntax visible, or hide formatting characters for a cleaner, more minimal writing experience. This lets you keep the flexibility of Markdown while enjoying an editor that feels closer to a plain, distraction-free writing space.

  • Browse local files on your device.
  • Open and edit files stored in iCloud.
  • Minimal Markdown editing for iPhone and iPad.
  • Tap to toggle tasks.
  • Long press to quickly copy text from code blocks.
  • Option to hide Markdown syntax for a cleaner look.
  • Custom themes to personalise the editor.
  • Font choices for a writing experience that feels right.
  • Simple interface designed around focus and readability.
  • Great for notes, drafts, documentation, journals and plain text workflows.

Supporter Upgrade

Downward also includes an optional one-time supporter upgrade. Supporters unlock extra themes, the ability to import and export custom themes, and the option to import their own fonts.

Your support helps keep Downward independent, maintained and improving over time.
