Done..

Your todo.txt, beautifully simple..

A clean, calm task list for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built on a plain text file you own.

Done.. puts a tidy, Reminders-style list on top of a todo.txt file. The text file is the source of truth: Done.. reads it, shows it as a list and writes your changes straight back. No database, no sync service and no account.

Choose any folder in Files, such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or On My iPhone, or simply keep your tasks on the device. Done.. creates todo.txt and done.txt there if they don't already exist, and because they're just text, every other todo.txt app and editor you use can open them too. Done.. is careful with your file: lines it doesn't understand are left exactly as they were, and changes made by other apps are never overwritten.

  • Works with your own todo.txt and done.txt.
  • Keep your tasks in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, any folder in Files, or on your device.
  • Plays nicely with other todo.txt apps and devices.
  • Colour-coded priorities.
  • Due dates, with today and overdue in red.
  • +projects and @contexts shown as tags.
  • Group by priority, project, context or due date, and sort by priority, due date or title.
  • Add several tasks at once, one per line.
  • Archive completed tasks to done.txt, and restore them any time.
  • Undo for deleting and archiving.
  • Optional creation dates on new tasks.
  • No account. No data collected.
  • 100% made with Swift and SwiftUI.

Tip Jar

Done.. is free, with nothing locked away. If you enjoy it, the optional Tip Jar on iPhone and iPad is a way to say thanks. Tips don't unlock anything, but they help keep Done.. independent, maintained and improving over time.
