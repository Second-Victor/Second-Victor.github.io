Dinkus

Your plain text, nicely coloured..

A simple text editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with minimal syntax highlighting.

Dinkus edits your files where they are. No library to import into, no database and no account. It colours your Markdown and JSON as you write, but the markup stays visible and the colouring never changes your text.

On iPhone and iPad, choose a folder on your device, in iCloud Drive or anywhere else in Files, then browse its subfolders and create, rename, move or delete files without leaving the app. On Mac, Dinkus works like TextEdit: open files from Finder, with window tabs, autosave, Versions and Open Recent.

Dinkus is careful with your files. It notices when one changes in another app or on another device, and if there's a conflict it asks which version you want to keep.

And the name? A dinkus is the * * * that marks a pause between sections of writing. It's small and quiet, and it knows when to stop.

  • Edit plain text, Markdown and JSON files.
  • Colour for Markdown and JSON, with the markup always visible.
  • Browse a folder and its subfolders on iPhone and iPad.
  • Keep your files on your device, in iCloud Drive or anywhere else in Files.
  • Finder, window tabs, autosave and Versions on Mac.
  • Tap to toggle tasks on iPhone and iPad.
  • Return carries on your task lists.
  • Optional line numbers.
  • Dinkus Dark and Dinkus Light themes, or import your own as JSON.
  • A choice of fonts, including your installed fonts on Mac.
  • Spelling, smart quotes, smart dashes and text replacement settings on Mac.
  • No account. No data collected.
  • 100% made with Swift.

Tip Jar

Dinkus is free, with nothing locked away. If you enjoy it, the optional Tip Jar is a way to say thanks. Tips don't unlock anything, but they help keep Dinkus independent, maintained and improving over time.
